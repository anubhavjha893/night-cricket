MANUAL WHATSAPP MESSAGES - 20 NOVEMBER 2026

ADMIN FLOW
Every registration row has a WhatsApp button next to its phone number. It opens
a compact modal with Registration Approved / Registration Rejected choices.
Select a choice to preview the personalised draft, then explicitly Open WhatsApp.
Player details also provide a Send message action. A successful approval or
rejection offers the matching draft automatically in a confirmation modal;
No, not right now / Escape closes the modal without undoing that saved decision.
Selecting a message from the list never changes the registration status.

The modal follows the site's colours, blurs its backdrop, traps keyboard focus,
restores focus on dismissal and fits desktop/mobile screens. An unset auction
date is clearly shown as Jald announce ki jayegi; an actual date is never invented.
Admin > Payment & settings > Auction date persists a validated calendar date in
MongoDB. Older settings clients that omit the field preserve the saved date.
Admin middleware protects settings reads/writes; players and anonymous callers
cannot set the date. Existing approval/rejection and in-app notifications remain.

APPROVAL DRAFT (personalised from registration and tournament data)
Namaste {name}, congratulations! {tournamentName} mein aapka registration
successfully verify aur approve ho gaya hai.
Auction date: {admin-configured auction date}.
Auction ke baad aapko aapki team aur team owner ki details bata di jayengi.
-- Tournament Organising Committee

REJECTION DRAFT
Namaste {name}, {tournamentName} ke liye aapka registration approve nahi ho saka,
kyunki aapka payment receive/verify nahi hua hai ya payment screenshot sahi ya
saaf nahi hai.
Kripya apna payment check karke sahi aur readable payment screenshot organiser
ko WhatsApp par bhejein, taaki dobara verification ki ja sake. Sahayata ke liye
organiser se sampark karein.
-- Tournament Organising Committee

MANUAL SENDING AND NUMBER VALIDATION
Only https://wa.me/<international-number>?text=<URL-encoded-message> is used.
No WhatsApp Business API, paid service, automatic sending or bulk workflow.
The organiser presses Send in their own WhatsApp account. Opening a draft does
not write a sent/delivered status or create an additional in-app notification.
10-digit Indian numbers gain 91 once; already-prefixed 91, +91, 0091 numbers
are normalized without duplication. Spaces, parentheses and hyphens are accepted.
Invalid numbers show a clear error and no Open WhatsApp link.
Names, tournament name/season and special characters are encoded into the URL.
Reference: https://faq.whatsapp.com/5913398998672934
