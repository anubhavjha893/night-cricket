REGISTRATION AND IMAGE UPDATE - 20 NOVEMBER 2026

New registration forms use the entered mobile number as the password. The
read-only password preview mirrors changes immediately. The server derives the
password from its normalized phone field when no separate password is supplied.
Hashes remain bcrypt; organiser authentication and existing player passwords
are unchanged. Compatibility for already-open older forms is retained.

This phone-as-password behavior was explicitly requested by the owner. It is
predictable authentication: anyone knowing a player's number can sign in as
that player and access their private profile and payment screenshot. Rate
limits cannot resolve that fundamental privacy risk. OTP or a private password
would be needed to restore proof of ownership. No existing account is reset.

The new form does not collect UTR or transaction reference. A payment screenshot
and organiser verification remain required. Old references and their unique
index are retained for compatible rollout. New records receive a hidden unique
NOT-COLLECTED marker for that old index; it is not a payment reference. Neither
player nor organiser screens display a reference field. Phone uniqueness and
authenticated replay keep ordinary retries from duplicating registrations or
uploaded files. Older clients supplying real references retain duplicate checks.

GET /health and /api/health return HTTP 200, text/plain, exactly:
health is good
These are liveness endpoints, even if MongoDB is unavailable. /api/ready remains
the separate database readiness check used by Render. Vercel forwards /health
and /api/* to the backend.
