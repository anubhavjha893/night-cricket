HEALTH IMPLEMENTATION
/health and /api/health now use the same bounded Atlas ping as /api/ready.
Connected DB + successful ping: 200 text/plain, exactly health is good.
Disconnected DB, failed ping or draining server: 503, Retry-After: 10.
No secrets, personal records or internal errors are returned.
Responses are no-store at the backend and Vercel edge. Mixed concurrent health/
readiness checks share one ping per second; each new monitoring interval performs
an actual dependency check instead of returning a cached static success.
The existing Render platform health check stays on /api/ready.

WAITING EXPERIENCE
Initial configuration and session checks are allowed to finish normally. If
startup takes over 1.2 seconds, a full-screen branded waiting dialog appears.
It does not unmount the existing page/form. It closes automatically after both
checks succeed; an ordinary signed-out 401 counts as a successful session check.
A failed config/session check shows Retry connection. A successful retry restores
the original page. Keyboard focus is held within the waiting UI and restored
afterwards. Mobile layout and reduced-motion preferences are supported.
Once initial startup has completed, routine settings refreshes do not block the
whole website or reset an open form. Existing upload progress remains unchanged.
Previous bounded startup GET retries remain enabled; writes are not auto-replayed.

LIMITS AND OWNERSHIP
The owner will configure UptimeRobot; no monitor or keep-alive job was silently
created. Regular incoming HTTP requests are documented Render activity, but no
monitor guarantees uptime, stops a provider outage, resumes a manually paused
Atlas cluster or prevents connection/billing/quota failures.
Render Free sleeps after 15 idle minutes and shares 750 free instance hours per
workspace per calendar month. Keeping one service continuously active uses most
of that allowance; other free services in the workspace share the same quota.
Exhausting it can suspend free services. No paid plan was purchased or changed.
Atlas Free can auto-pause after 30 days without activity. The monitor verifies a
real driver ping, but Atlas account permissions, backups, cluster configuration,
manual pause/resume and availability remain the owner's responsibility.
For required production uptime, use an owner-approved always-on compute plan
and appropriate Atlas availability/backup settings; monitoring is not failover.
